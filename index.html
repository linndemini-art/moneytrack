Privacy Policy – MoneyTrack – Smart Money

Effective date: October 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how MoneyTrack – Smart Money ("MoneyTrack", "the app", "we", or "our") handles information when you use the application. Our aim is to help you manage your finances while keeping your financial records under your control.

1. Information Stored by the App

MoneyTrack allows you to record and manage financial information, which may include income, expenses, transaction descriptions, categories, monthly balances, scheduled items, savings goals, and related progress.

This information is stored locally on your device. MoneyTrack does not operate its own account system or cloud synchronization service for these records. The app does not send these financial records to a server operated by the developer.

2. Information Collection and Sharing

MoneyTrack is designed to function without requiring you to provide your name, create an account, or submit financial records to the developer. The app does not intentionally collect or transmit your financial records for advertising, analytics, or tracking purposes.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share financial records stored by the app with the developer or other third parties through an app-operated online service.

If you contact us by email, we will receive the information you choose to include in your message, such as your email address and the contents of your inquiry. We use that information to respond and handle the inquiry.

3. Backups and Exported Files

MoneyTrack provides an export or backup feature so you can create a copy of your financial records. When you choose to export a backup, the resulting file is saved using the destination or file-handling options available on your device.

The app does not automatically upload exported backup files to a developer operated server. If you choose to copy a backup to a cloud storage service, send it to another person, or save it somewhere else, that transfer and the destination's privacy practices are outside MoneyTrack's control.

A backup file may contain sensitive financial information. Keep it in a secure location and share it only with people or services you trust.

4. Data Storage, Security, and Retention

Financial records remain on your device until you delete them, clear the app's stored data, uninstall the app, or otherwise remove them. The precise behavior may depend on your device and Android settings.

You are responsible for protecting access to your device and any backup files you create. Device security features, such as a screen lock, can help protect locally stored information. No method of electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

MoneyTrack does not maintain a separate server-side copy of your financial records that the developer can delete on your behalf. Files exported or copied outside the app may remain after you delete the original records or uninstall MoneyTrack. You must remove those copies separately.

5. Permissions and Device Features

MoneyTrack may use Android system features to support app functionality, including saving or exporting backup files. Any access to device features is intended to support the relevant function you use. You can review app permissions through your Android device's settings.

6. Third-Party Services

MoneyTrack is designed without third-party advertising or analytics services. If a future version introduces third-party services that affect personal information handling, this Privacy Policy will be updated as appropriate.

If you obtain MoneyTrack through an app store, that store may process information under its own privacy policy. Such processing is separate from the local financial records managed by MoneyTrack.

7. Children's Privacy

MoneyTrack is not designed specifically for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through an app-operated online collection system.

8. Your Choices

You can manage or delete records using the app's available features. Clearing MoneyTrack's app data or uninstalling the app may permanently remove locally stored records. Consider exporting a backup first if you want to preserve them.

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how MoneyTrack handles information, you can contact us using the details below.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the app's features or data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page, and the effective date will be updated when appropriate.
